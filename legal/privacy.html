Privacy Policy

Data Controller: Artist Life Foundation (AIL), operating OCRA · Last updated: 20 October 2026 · ODPC Registration: In progress (tracked as open item per OCRA_STANDARDS.md)

OCRA ("we", "us") is a career and results analytics platform operated as a subsidiary of Artist Life Foundation. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and your rights under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and ODPC guidance.

1. What we collect · data minimisation

We collect only what is necessary to provide OCRA's core service: analysing academic records and cognitive signals to suggest career paths. We do not collect data "just in case".

2. Children's data · strict protections

OCRA is used by learners aged 6 to adult. For any learner under 18:

  • We require verified guardian or parent consent before processing. For Personal accounts declaring age under 13, creation is blocked and a Family account with guardian verification is required.
  • Student records are never public, never indexed by search engines, never in URLs or logs, and never shared across tenants (schools/families).
  • Accommodation information (e.g., learning support needs) is collected only with separate, explicit, clickable consent, never shown on the default dashboard, never used for scoring, and never shared across tenants.
  • Cohort comparisons are opt-in, anonymised, and aggregated · no named classmates, ever.
  • Guardian rights: access, correct, delete, or withdraw consent for a child's data at any time. See Section 7.

3. How we use AI (Claude)

We use Anthropic's Claude API to help draft insights and career suggestions. To protect you:

  • We never send direct identifiers (names, phone, school, guardian contacts, photos) to Claude. Data is pseudonymised · only the minimum needed for the task.
  • We never send accommodation data to Claude.
  • Every AI-generated report includes a clear in-product disclosure: "This is AI-assisted analysis to support your decisions. It is not a guarantee of career outcomes or professional advice. Review with a teacher or counsellor." plus an explanation of why each career was suggested.
  • The "career backdoor" (typing a career name) uses web-search grounding, validates output against a strict schema, stores results as auto-generated, pending review with a 60-day "New" label, and shows a Developer review queue before it affects scoring.

6. Security

We protect data with: argon2id/bcrypt password hashing, encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), Row Level Security on every database table with strict tenant isolation (your school or family never sees another's data), rate limiting on login and payments, CSRF/XSS protection, CSP/HSTS headers, and immutable audit logs. Student records are session-locked and never appear in URLs or application logs. See our security page for the current audit date.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach that risks your rights, we will notify the ODPC within 72 hours and you without delay, as required by the DPA.

7. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act you have the right to:

  • Access · get a copy of your data (Settings → Export My Data, or email us)
  • Correct · fix inaccurate data
  • Delete · request deletion (we honour this within 30 days, except where law requires us to keep payment records)
  • Object or restrict · object to certain processing
  • Portability · receive your data in a usable format
  • Withdraw consent · at any time (does not affect processing already done)
  • Complain · to us first, then to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), complaints@odpc.go.ke

How to act: Email privacy@ocra.app or use in-app Settings. We respond within 30 days. For a child's data, the verified guardian makes the request.

8. Consent on every form

Every form that collects personal data shows a clear checkbox with a link to this policy and the Terms. You must tick it to proceed. For organisational sign-up, there is a per-section agreement so you know what each section will do with the data. Pre-ticked boxes are never used. You can withdraw consent at any time.
