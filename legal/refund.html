Refund Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Operator: Artist Life Foundation, OCRA · Contact: support@ocra.app, +254 700 000 000 · Kenya Consumer Protection Act 2012 applies

OCRA charges KES 40 for each document (Academic Transcript, Results Report, Career Guidance, Revision Timetable). Each payment unlocks one document for 30 days from payment. No subscription, no auto-renewal, no hidden fees. Re-downloading within the same 30-day window does not charge again.

Price is server-decided. What you see before you pay is what you pay. M-Pesa confirmation and your OCRA receipt will show the same KES 40. If they differ, you are entitled to a refund.

1. When you are eligible for a refund

SituationEligible?What happens
You were charged but the document did not unlock (network failure, M-Pesa callback missed)Yes · alwaysWe verify the M-Pesa transaction code on Daraja, unlock the document and extend 30 days from verification. If you prefer money back, we refund KES 40.
Duplicate charge for the same document in the same 30-day window (e.g., tapped Pay twice)YesRefund of the duplicate KES 40 within 5 business days.
You paid the wrong document (e.g., bought Timetable but needed Transcript)Yes, within 24 hours if you have not downloaded or printed the wrong documentWe move the unlock to the correct document or refund and you re-purchase. After download/print, no refund.
You changed your mind after downloading or printingNoDigital goods consumed on download/print are not refundable under Kenya CPA s56(2). You keep access for the remaining 30 days.
Report has a factual error (wrong name, missing subject, wrong scores we entered incorrectly)YesWe fix the report at no extra charge and extend access 7 days. If we cannot fix within 48 hours, refund KES 40.
You disagree with AI-assisted career suggestionsNoThese are guidance, not guarantees (see Terms s2). We include an explanation for each suggestion. Talk to a teacher/counsellor · we do not refund for differing opinions.

2. How to request a refund

1
Gather: M-Pesa transaction code (e.g., QGH...), phone used, document name, and what went wrong.
2
Contact us: Email support@ocra.app with subject "Refund · [Transaction Code]" or use in-app Help → Report a payment issue. Phone/WhatsApp: +254 700 000 000.
3
We verify: We check the Daraja callback and payment audit trail (idempotent). We respond within 48 hours (business days) with approval or a clear reason.
4
Money back: Approved refunds are reversed via M-Pesa B2C to the same phone within 5 business days. You get confirmation by SMS and email.

3. What you will receive

  • An email/SMS acknowledgment within 24 hours of your request.
  • A decision within 48 hours (business days) · approved, or declined with specific reason and how to appeal.
  • For approved M-Pesa refunds: funds to the same phone number within 5 business days. For card refunds (where offered): to the original card within 7 to 10 business days (bank dependent).
  • An updated receipt showing the reversal.

4. Appeals

If you disagree with a refund decision, reply to the same email thread and ask for a senior review. A different team member reviews within 5 business days. You retain all rights under the Kenya Consumer Protection Act to escalate to the CAK (Competition Authority of Kenya) or the courts.

5. Fraud, abuse, and chargebacks

We verify every callback origin and payload, use idempotent handlers so the same M-Pesa code cannot credit twice, and keep a full payment audit trail. Fraudulent refund claims, chargeback abuse, or tampering with serials/QR codes may lead to account suspension and reporting to Safaricom and law enforcement. Genuine mistakes are always handled with goodwill.

6. Developer and Organisation accounts

Developer accounts are internal and not charged. Organisation bulk purchases (where offered) are invoiced separately · their refund window and method are on the invoice.
