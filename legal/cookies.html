Cookies and Similar Technologies Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · This policy is part of our Privacy Policy

OCRA uses cookies and similar technologies (localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB) to make the site work, keep you signed in, and (only with your consent) understand how it is used. This policy explains what we store and how to control it.

Our commitment: We ask for your consent before any non-essential script runs. Strictly necessary storage works without consent; analytics and marketing storage only runs if you choose "Accept". You can change your choice at any time in Settings or via the banner that reappears after 6 months.

1. What are these technologies?

  • Cookies · small text files your browser stores and sends back to us. Some last for a session, others until expiry.
  • localStorage / sessionStorage · browser storage OCRA uses to keep your session and preferences. The Privacy Policy treats these the same as cookies under Kenya DPA guidance.
  • We do not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or fingerprinting.

4. Your choices

When you first visit OCRA, a banner at the bottom asks:

  • Accept all · necessary + analytics
  • Reject non-essential · necessary only
  • Manage · choose per category

You can change this at any time: Settings → Privacy → Cookie preferences, or clear site data in your browser. Withdrawing analytics consent does not affect necessary cookies already set for your session.

5. How to control via your browser

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary storage, OCRA will not be able to keep you signed in and many features will not work. Instructions:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies

6. Do Not Track and consent signals

We respect Global Privacy Control (GPC) where sent by your browser as a request not to enable non-essential storage. We do not sell personal data.

7. Children's consent

For learners under 18, the guardian's consent controls analytics. A child cannot enable analytics without guardian approval.

8. Contact and updates

Questions about cookies? Email privacy@ocra.app or dpo@ocra.app. Material changes to this policy trigger a new consent banner. Last reviewed date is at the top.
